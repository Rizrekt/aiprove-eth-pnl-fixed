A note on process: the first sweep used slippage 0.5 * participation
(a bps/fraction mix-up, 100x too harsh); every active strategy looked
catastrophic. Caught because buy-and-hold slippage was implausible,
corrected to 0.005 * participation (50 bps at 100% participation) and
the whole sweep re-run. All numbers below are from the corrected model.

== 3. Caveats stated plainly ==
- The challenge publishes NO harness, NO fee schedule and NO strategy
  interface (checked: challenge README, full repo tree, live API detail
  for slug eth-pnl-fixed, 2026-11-14). The cost model above is an
  explicit assumption set (Binance-spot-like), and the strategy is
  profitable under every stress combination tested - that robustness,
  not the point estimate, is the claim.
- PnL is in USD on a fixed 10 ETH position; if the sandbox sizes
  positions differently, strategy.py's SIZE_ETH is the single knob.
